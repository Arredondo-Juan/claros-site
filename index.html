How it works

Everything happens in the browser, for the office and the specialist. Nothing to install.

Send the scan.

Export the CBCT or pano from the imaging software you already use and drag it into Claros. Pick the specialist, add a note, and send.

Big files pick up where they left off if the connection drops.

They open it in 3D.

The specialist opens the scan with a one-time code and sees it in full 3D, right in their browser. No account, no software, and they never pay. You see when it's opened, and their reply comes back to you.

Clear reads, next. Coming next

Questions

Questions

What scans can we send? CBCT scans and panoramic X-rays, exported from your imaging software as DICOM files.

Does the specialist need an account? No. They open the scan with a one-time code, see it in 3D in their browser, and never pay.

Do we need new software or equipment? No. Claros runs in a web browser. Keep your scanner and imaging software, export the scan, and upload it.

Who reads our scans? Today, you send each scan to the specialist you choose. Claros radiology reads launch once licensed oral and maxillofacial radiologists are signed on.

Is patient information protected? Before any real patient scan is sent, we sign a business associate agreement with your office.

Is a read a diagnosis? No. A read supports your diagnosis. The treating dentist always makes the call.

What does the pilot ask of us? Use Claros on real cases and tell us what's confusing or missing.

What happens after 90 days? You move to founder pricing, $99 a month, and keep it for as long as you stay.

We have several offices. Can we join? Yes. We'd like to hear what each office needs.
